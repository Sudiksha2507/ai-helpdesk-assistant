Title: Forgot Password / Account Locked
Steps:
1. Go to the login page and click "Forgot Password".
2. Enter your registered email address to receive a reset link.
3. Check your spam folder if the email doesn't arrive within 5 minutes.
4. Choose a new password with at least 8 characters, including a number.
5. If your account is locked after failed attempts, wait 15 minutes before retrying.
